# FetanAgent language policy

## Version 1 product language

FetanAgent is English-only in version 1. This applies to every FetanAgent-created, user-visible
artifact:

- customer Telegram messages and bot menus;
- Owner/Admin dashboard pages, validation messages, notifications, and audit reason-code labels;
- website and API documentation intended for people;
- downloadable plans, guides, invoices generated by FetanAgent, PDFs, and other exported files;
- support templates, release notes, and help content.

Do not add a language selector, a per-user locale preference, or an Amharic copy of product text
in version 1. The shared locale contract is fixed to `en`.

## Data and evidence are not interface translations

This policy does not change user-provided or provider-provided data. Names, bank/payment evidence,
SMS text, screenshots, and PDFs may legitimately contain Amharic or another language. They remain
evidence or identity data and must not be translated, overwritten, or rejected merely because of
their language. Payment verification still requires authoritative provider evidence rather than
text translation or OCR alone.

## Future artifacts

There are currently no tracked FetanAgent PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, slide decks, or
other downloadable documents. When one is created, its FetanAgent-written content must be English
and it must be checked before delivery. Supporting a new product language later requires an
explicit product decision, updated copy, tests, and a reviewed data migration if a locale field is
introduced.
